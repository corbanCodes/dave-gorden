The Idea

Everyone is a guest. Everyone.

Most customer-service programs start at the front counter. Guest Astonishment starts in the boardroom — because a team that isn’t treated properly will never treat your customers properly. It isn’t a script or a smile campaign. It’s a way of running the whole house.

Inside the house

The Internal Guest

Your team members. The way leadership treats them — orients them, listens to them, re‑orients them — sets the ceiling for everything your customers will ever experience.

Through the front door

The External Guest

Your customers. Satisfaction keeps them quiet. Astonishment keeps them coming back — and telling everyone they know on the way.

“The mind, once stretched, never returns to its original size.”
The principle behind every program
What He Does

Four ways in — none of them off a shelf.

Every program is built from what Dave found inside your operation. He is an inspirational speaker, not a motivational one: the difference is whether it wears off on the drive home.

01

Guest Astonishment

3–5 hours · leadership first

Hands-on working sessions tailored to your industry and your people. The C‑suite goes first, because the Internal Guest sets the ceiling for the External Guest. Then the idea moves through every team they lead.

02

The Mystery Guest

Undercover · C‑suite debrief

Dave experiences your business the way your guests do — a shopper in the aisle, a diner at the table, even a patient admitted for three days. Then he sits down with leadership and tells them everything he found.

03

Re-Orientation

Small groups · recurring

Orientation happens once, the day someone is hired, and then it fades. Re‑Orientation is the reset: multi-day engagements in small groups that renew the standard. Clients bring it back yearly.

04

DISC Team Profiles

Assessment · individual reports

An online assessment ahead of the program; a fifteen-page report in every participant’s hands the day of. Dominant, Influential, Stable, Compliant — the ideal team blends all four, and Dave shows yours where it leans.

How It Begins

Prescription without diagnosis is malpractice.

Before Dave recommends anything, he spends two days inside your organization — not in a conference room, in the operation.

Evening One

Dinner with the president

The first evening belongs to leadership. Dave listens to what they believe is happening in their house.

Day Two

Inside the operation

The floor, the routes, the back of the house — the places the org chart doesn’t show. Whatever your people do, he watches it done.

Breakfast

What he found

Before he leaves, he tells leadership exactly what he would build. If it sounds right, the program moves forward.

Across Industries

Clients bring him back, year after year.

Astonishment belongs everywhere people are served, and Dave has carried it into most of them — hotel groups, hospital systems, banks, national food brands, and the family business down the road.
