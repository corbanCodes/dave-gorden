About Dave

Inspirational. Not motivational.

There’s a difference. Motivation wears off somewhere on the drive home. Dave’s job is to stretch the way your people think — and the mind, once stretched, never returns to its original size.

Dave GordenKnoxville, Tennessee

How He Works

The speaker who rides the routes.

Before Dave ever took the stage for a national snack-food company, he rode the delivery routes with their drivers — two full runs, learning how they were paid, what they carried, and what a good day actually looked like. By the time he stood up in front of the room, he knew their business the way they did.

For a greeting-card giant, he went ahead of every program — walking the stores, talking with the people behind the counters — until the client said he knew the product line as well as the people selling it.

That’s the pattern. Some speakers do two hours and a firewalk and are gone by dinner. Dave is still there the next morning, asking your route drivers questions. Every program is tailored to the client in front of him — planned, not canned — and it starts where the culture starts: with the C‑suite.

Where the Work Has Taken Him

Five continents. Every kind of house.

Hotels and resorts. Hospitals with mystery-patient programs. Financial organizations. National food and retail brands. And the mom-and-pop operations down the road, which get the same standard without the travel.

The philosophy
